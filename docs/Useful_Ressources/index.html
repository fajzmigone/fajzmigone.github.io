Survey Sampling

ENSEA, Abidjan · Semester 2 · 2026–2027 · 25 hours · 2.5 credits

This course develops the methodological foundations needed to design sample surveys while accounting for the practical constraints of fieldwork, prior information, and the study setting.

The syllabus covers:

  • survey design, data-collection methods, and sources of survey error (2 hours);
  • simple random sampling, estimation, precision, and sample selection (5 hours);
  • non-probability methods, including quota, route, typical-unit, and volunteer sampling (6 hours); and
  • designs using auxiliary information: unequal probabilities, stratification, multistage sampling, and cluster sampling (12 hours).

Assessment is based on one written examination.
